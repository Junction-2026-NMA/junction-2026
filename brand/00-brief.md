## The challenge

Junction 2026, FinTech track, by S-Pankki: **Personal Economic Trainer**.

> Build an AI experience that lets people chat with their own money, turning personal banking data into a trusted financial coach.

What S-Pankki says it values (the closest thing to judging criteria):

1. **Three spending baskets** as the teaching angle: mandatory and regular (rent), mandatory and irregular (a broken washing machine), and everyday spending that slips through your fingers (coffees).
2. **MyData spirit**: users explore their own data freely, not fixed bank-picked charts.
3. **Financial literacy, especially for young adults.**
4. Optional open data (Statistics Finland) for benchmarks.

Output: a mobile app, or a web app that can sit inside a mobile app. Data: a synthetic transaction dataset modelled on real S-Pankki data.

The brief text comes from junction2026.com, which is not confirmed as the official source. Check it against what S-Pankki hands out at the venue.

## What the brief does not ask for

It does not ask for a rebrand, an age range or an income limit. Those are our choices. The brand has to serve the chat, the baskets and free exploration, and it must keep the word **trusted** true.

## Our product in one paragraph

**Hillo** (working name) is a money coach inside S-Pankki for 18 to 35 year olds who earn under €2,000 net a month. You chat with your own transactions through Liito, a flying squirrel mascot. Liito sorts your spending into three baskets, tells you what is safe to spend this week, finds the small leaks, and turns each finding into one tiny daily action. Small actions keep a streak, earn berries and fill savings jars. It feels like Duolingo, and it runs on real bank data.

| Choice | Decision |

## Facts to get right in the pitch

- S-Pankki is **not** the biggest bank in Finland. It has 3.5 million customers (914,000 active) and about 3.4 % of loans and 4.9 % of deposits. OP and Nordea are far larger.
- What is true and strong: S-mobiili has about 2.9 million users and is used weekly by about one in three Finnish adults, and S-Pankki leads Finnish banks on brand esteem and customer satisfaction.
- S-Pankki already has the saving rails: **Säästäjä** saves a small sum on each card purchase. It has over 130,000 users, but only 11 % are 18 to 25. The missing piece is motivation for young people. That is our pitch.
