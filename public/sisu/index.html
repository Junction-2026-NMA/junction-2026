Good to know.

Straight answers before you try it.

Is Sisu a real S-Pankki service?

No. Sisu is a concept built at Junction 2026 for S-Pankki's challenge. It runs on synthetic data. It is not a service that S-Pankki offers.

Where does the data come from?

From synthetic data that S-Pankki provided for the challenge. No real customer's transactions are in it.

What does Sisu cost?

Nothing. The concept is free for customers, and nothing is locked behind payment.

Can spending break my streak?

No. The streak never depends on not spending, so buying something cannot break it. One tiny action a day keeps it going.

Does Sisu sell loans or credit?

No. Sisu never sells or markets loans, credit or pay-later, and it earns nothing from debt.
