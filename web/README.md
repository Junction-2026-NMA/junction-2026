# Sisu web port: shared conventions

## Status and how to check it (rework integration, 2026-11-15)

The iOS app is ported (gate, onboarding, picker, Today, the Sisu conversation, Path, the voice lab, all drawing the same `public/rive/sisu_ui.riv`) and reworked on the web for the Junction demo. **Three tabs: Today, Sisu and Path.** Sisu (it was Talk) is the page the app opens on. Since the rework (2026-11-15) there are no berries, streaks, reward counters, daily actions, Jars screen or jar illustrations anywhere; the Rive file and its contract are unchanged (their berry and jar artboards are simply not used).

**The structure since the rework:**

- **Sisu tab** (`web/features/Talk`): the conversation and a pinned ask box show at once (no extra sheet to start typing). Typed and spoken questions go into one session, so switching keeps the context. Header: the Sisu logo top left ("Open chat history", a dot when a project has unread findings), the title, the live voice status, and once chatting a small Liito badge with the task moods. Empty: a larger Liito and suggested questions that work today (`web/core/AppLogic/SisuSuggestions.ts`). The transcript keeps the reading position: a new question scrolls to the top once, and "Jump to latest" appears when there is more below (`TalkTranscript.tsx`). Hands-free voice turns the ask box into a live strip (Listening, Hearing you, Thinking, Answering; `VoiceStrip.tsx`, `web/core/AppLogic/VoiceBar.ts`).
- **Chat history drawer** (`web/features/History`): New chat, previous conversations of the customer (kept on this device, `web/core/ConversationHistory.ts`, `Keys.conversations`; reopening one restores its context with `CoachSession.restore`), **Saved projects**, and a **Settings** row at the bottom.
- **Projects** (`web/features/Projects`, store `web/app/ProjectStore.ts`): ongoing watches the person approved in a conversation (`WatchActionCard`, "Would you like me to keep checking…?"). The drawer lists them (goal, status, last successful check, unread findings); one opens **in place of the conversation** (`openProjectId`; the conversation stays mounted and hidden under it). The project view shows checks said honestly (a schedule is promised only when the server names one), requirements (every field `PATCH /api/watch/:id` takes, checked in place, when the backend has it; else a note on how to change them), pages watched, the homes that fit now (`GET /api/watch/:id` `currentMatches`) and the findings, each with the housing effect, Open listing and a viewing enquiry to copy (never sent), and pause/resume/stop. Which routes exist is read from the list (`scheduler`, `projectStatus`), never probed. MainScreen keeps the store polling (`useProjectsPolling`, a check every 60 s while the page is visible and a project is active) and opens `?project=<id>` (`useProjectLaunch`).
- **Answers** (`web/features/Ask`, `web/core/AnswerPresentation.ts`): one card with the text, one result with the most useful number and its source chips (Bank data, Your input, Web with provider and time, Estimate, Simulated, Mock data, synthetic demo data comparison), then "Details" closed: Offers compared, How this was worked out, Assumptions, Sources (each a `Disclosure`). Nothing is repeated across them. An offer that misses the person's requirements is never "Best match" and is listed apart, with what it misses but no cost or saving; when none qualifies the card says "No matching options found" with "Broaden the search" and "Keep looking". Unknown fees stay "unknown", never €0. Simulated actions say so ("Simulated · no real switch").
- **Today** (`web/features/Today`): what needs attention at a glance. The money overview with the **Estimated weekly budget** (`web/core/AppLogic/WeeklyBudget.ts`: (balance − bills due before payday − buffer) ÷ weeks to payday, never below 0, at least one week; never called "safe to spend"), the balance, payday and the data date; one primary insight (`TodayFocus.primary`: a projected shortfall is the one warning); three basket buttons (Regular essentials, Occasional essentials, Everyday spending; amounts only after a tap, peer figures labelled as the synthetic demo data); the newest payments with "See all". "How this is worked out" (MathsSheet) shows every step and one realistic adjustment. The buffer is `prefs.bufferEur` or a default marked "(assumed)": the backend's own default when `CashFlow.weeklyBudget` sends one (one week of regular and occasional essentials), else €100.
- **Path** (`web/features/Path`): "N of 7 lessons" and a progress bar. Lessons unlock in order (a locked one says why). A lesson completes only with a right answer: a wrong one gets a kind explanation (`whyWrong` when the backend sends it) and one follow-up question (`followUp`, else `web/core/AppLogic/LessonFollowUps.ts`). "Ask Sisu" inside a lesson saves the place (`sisu.lessonAttempt.<customer>`) and reopens it on return.
- **Settings** (`web/features/Sheets/SettingsSheet.tsx`, opened from Today, Path and the Sisu drawer): Voice and language ("I speak", "Sisu answers in", voices with a Play preview, Speak replies), Your buffer, Data and privacy, and collapsed Demo controls. The same voice step is in onboarding (skippable). Changing them keeps conversations and projects.
- **Prefs** (`web/app/AppPrefs.ts`, `sisu.prefs`: language, voiceId, speakReplies, bufferEur) and `web/voice/VoicePrefs.ts` (`sisu.voiceLanguages`; `VoicePrefs.recogniserLanguage()` is sent with both push-to-talk and hands-free voice turns).
- **Texture and motion**: a low-opacity SVG grain on `.sisu-root` (`--sisu-texture`, off with "increase contrast"); screens, routes and the main container paint no background so it shows everywhere, cards and sheets stay solid. Motion tokens `--sisu-motion-enter/exit/press/sheet-*` with Reduce Motion values, classes `sisu-enter-soft` / `sisu-exit-soft` / `sisu-enter-fade`.
- **Backends.** The deployed app talks to today's hosted backend, which sends none of the newer fields; everything is feature-detected. Newer backend branches (rework/api-answers, rework/api-projects) are read where present: `Offer.feesUnknown` (and `feesKnown`), `greenEnergy`; `ResearchResult.noMatch`, `matchingCount`, `requirements` (`greenOnly`, `maxMonthlyCostEur` too), `requirementsText`; `CashFlow.weeklyBudget` (its default buffer); `PropertyWatch.projectStatus` (drawn over `status`), `WatchNotification.readAt` and `housing` (the housing effect: recurring, upfront, buffer, savings goal; `web/features/Projects/BackendHousing.ts`), `WatchList.scheduler` and `/api/health` `watchScheduler` (`"cron"` promises a daily review; missing means on-open). A project edit sends `{ customerId, budgetEur?, minRooms?, goal?, requirements? }`.
- **Starting over** ("Start the demo over" and `onboarding=reset`) clears names, old progress keys, preferences, voice languages, conversations, lesson progress and attempts, and seen findings (`clearPersonalData` in `web/app/AppState.ts`).

Conventions added at integration:

- **No CSS class shared between files.** Every CSS file is global, so a class name may be defined in one file only. Prefix with the feature (`sisu-cashflow-*` in Today, `sisu-chart-*` in Ask, `sisu-sheet-text` for sheet text vs `sisu-sheet-body` for the BottomSheet body). Check with: `for f in $(find web app/app -name '*.css'); do grep -oE '^\.[a-zA-Z0-9_-]+' $f | sort -u | sed "s|$| $f|"; done | sort | awk '{c[$1]++} END {for (k in c) if (c[k]>1) print k}'` (only `.sisu-root` may repeat). The global `.sisu-root button` reset outranks a single class, so button rules are written `button.sisu-x` or `.sisu-x[type]`.
- **Namespaces in web/core** mirror Swift's caseless enums; files that use them start with `/* eslint-disable @typescript-eslint/no-namespace -- ... */`.
- **BottomSheet medium detent:** at rest on medium, the part of the sheet below the screen edge is added as bottom padding to the body, so long content can still be scrolled to its end without dragging the sheet up.

## Gate + PWA (web/gate, app/app/layout.tsx, app/app/page.tsx)

`/app` decides what a visitor sees before the app mounts:

| Visitor | View |
|---|---|
| A computer (or a tablet) | `DesktopQr`: only "Scan this QR code with your phone to use the demo." and a QR code of `window.location.origin + "/app"` (drawn client-side with `qrcode` as SVG). |
| A phone in a normal browser tab | `InstallSteps`: add Sisu to the home screen, with steps for iOS Safari, other iOS browsers (Share > Add to Home Screen from iOS 16.4, else open in Safari), apps' built-in browsers (open in Safari/Chrome, copy link), Android Chrome/Edge/Samsung/Firefox (one-tap "Install Sisu" via `beforeinstallprompt` when offered, else the menu steps). A subtle "Continue in the browser" link lets people in anyway; that choice is kept in `sessionStorage` (`sisu.gate.continueInBrowser`) for the tab. |
| Launched from the home screen | The app (`web/app/SisuApp.tsx`, default export, loaded by `web/gate/AppLoader.tsx` with `next/dynamic` `ssr: false`). |

**Detection.** `app/app/page.tsx` (server) makes the first guess from the user-agent header (`userAgent` from `next/server`): a mobile UA is a phone, everything else (tablets included) a computer; `?source=homescreen` (the manifest `start_url`) is taken as a home-screen launch. `DeviceGate` renders that guess, then confirms on mount (`web/gate/clientDetect.ts`): phone = touch + coarse pointer + (mobile UA or an iPadOS-style Mac UA with touch) + short screen side <= 740 CSS px; standalone = `display-mode: standalone|fullscreen` or `navigator.standalone`. A guessed home-screen launch shows the plain launch background until the browser confirms, so the app never mounts in a tab that should show the steps. The decision itself is the pure `decideView()` in `web/gate/detect.ts`.

**Test overrides** (nothing is stored): `?device=phone` or `?device=desktop` forces the device kind; `?standalone=1` forces the in-app path (it wins over everything). Precedence: `standalone=1` > `device=desktop` > real home-screen launch > detected kind > "continue in browser".

**Shell.** `app/app/layout.tsx` wraps every `/app` page (the lab too) in `<div class="sisu-root {sisuFontVariables}">`, imports `web/theme/tokens.css` then `web/gate/shell.css`. `.sisu-root` is `position: fixed; inset: 0; height: 100dvh` and the document itself never scrolls or bounces (`html:has(.sisu-root)` gets `overflow: hidden; overscroll-behavior: none`). So: **the app must scroll inside its own containers** (give each scroller `overflow-y: auto; overscroll-behavior: contain`). `.sisu-root` scrolls only as a fallback for pages taller than the screen (the lab). The shell does **not** pad for safe areas: the app applies `env(safe-area-inset-*)` where it needs them (`viewport-fit=cover`; with `statusBarStyle: "default"` iOS keeps the status bar opaque, so the top inset is 0 when launched from the home screen). Double-tap zoom is off (`touch-action: manipulation` on everything, zero specificity, so a Rive canvas can still set `touch-action: none`), plus `maximumScale: 1`.

**PWA.** `public/app.webmanifest` (id `/app`, start_url `/app?source=homescreen`, scope `/app`, standalone, portrait, `#F6F8F7`), icons in `public/app-icons/` made with `sips` from the iOS `AppIcon-1024.png` (`icon-180.png` apple-touch, `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`, `maskable-512.png` = icon at 400 px padded to 512 with the icon's own green `#02A649`, so the face stays inside the maskable safe zone). **Service worker, push only (v2 push, 2026-11-15):** `public/sw.js`, registered at scope `/` (it covers the `/app` start_url) by `web/app/Push.ts` from `useProjectLaunch`. It has **no fetch handler**, so nothing is cached and updates during the demo stay instant. It shows each push (`{ title, body, url, tag }`, nothing financial; tag per project, renotify) and on a tap posts `{ type: "open-project", customerId, projectId }` to an open `/app` window (focused; `useProjectLaunch` switches the customer if needed and opens the project), else opens the push's `url` (`/app?source=homescreen&customer=&project=`). Notifications: Settings > Notifications and the project's "Background research" card (`web/features/Projects/PushControls.tsx`, `ProjectMonitoringStatus.tsx`) show Enabled / Off / Blocked in browser settings / "Add Sisu to your Home Screen to get notifications" (iOS outside the home-screen app) / Not supported here, with "Turn on notifications" (asks `Notification.requestPermission()` first thing in the tap, then subscribes and `POST /api/push/subscribe`) and "Send a test notification" (`POST /api/push/test`). Chrome on Android installs without a service worker anyway.

## web/core (part B): presentation, Rive contract, AppLogic

Ports of `Presentation.swift`, `ChartPresentation.swift`, `CoachPresentation.swift`, `RiveContract.swift` and the files of `AppLogic/` (same base names: `web/core/AppLogic/LessonPath.ts`, ...; the daily actions, streak and berries of `DailyProgress.swift` and `CoachRewards` were dropped on 2026-11-15, only `DayStamp` remains, in `AppLogic/DayStamp.ts`). Pure TypeScript, no React. Check: `npx tsx web/core/__checks__/core-b.check.ts` (ports the SisuCore tests' intent against the bundled fixtures; must print `0 failed`).

**Shape of the port.**
- A Swift `enum` of static functions (`Money`, `Dates`, `CoachText`, `TodayPresenter`, `RiveBindings`...) is a TS `namespace` with the same name and the same function names: `Money.format(64, "en")`, `TodayPresenter.flaggedBill(overview)`. Argument labels are dropped; arguments keep the Swift order, defaults stay at the end.
- A raw-value enum is a string union plus a namespace of the same name holding `allCases` and its methods: `BasketKind.label("fixed", "fi")`, `BasketKind.of(basketId)` (Swift `basketId.kind`).
- Nested Swift types are namespace members: `RiveBindings.LiitoMood`, `RiveBindings.ChoiceState`, `HeroViewState.Tone`, `LessonNode.State`, `ChartState.Body`. Flat aliases also exist (`LiitoMood`, `VoiceState`, ... from `RiveContract.ts`).
- Swift structs with `mutating` methods are **immutable** here: an interface plus functions that return the next value. `LessonProgress.complete(p, concept)` returns `{ progress, added }`; `OnboardingFlow.advance(flow)`, `TeachingQuiz.choose(quiz, i)` return the new state. Computed properties are functions taking the value (`OnboardingFlow.canSkip(flow)`, `TeachingQuiz.isCorrect(quiz)`). `VoiceClipQueue` is the one small mutable class (a player owns it).
- Stored state keeps Swift's Codable JSON shape (a `Set` is an array), so `JSON.stringify` it into storage and read it back with `LessonProgress.decode`, `OnboardingFlow.decode` (each returns `null` for anything invalid).
- Swift `nil` outputs are `null` (`string | null`); optional inputs accept `null` or `undefined`.
- Time zones are IANA strings; every `timeZone:` parameter defaults to `Dates.currentTimeZone()` (Swift `.current`). Days: `DayStamp.from(new Date())`.
- Money and dates are formatted by hand exactly like Swift (not `Intl`), so the strings match iOS character for character: `€1,234`, `1 234 €` with U+00A0, a real minus U+2212, halves away from zero (`roundAway`).

**Where to find things.**

- `CoachPresentation.ts`: `CoachText`, `OfferPresenter`, `CardLine`, `StagePresenter`, `ActionPresenter` (also the `warning`/`confirmation`/`destination` that Swift adds in ChartPresentation.swift), `GroundingPresenter`, `ToolPresenter`, `TeachingQuiz`, `FuturesPresenter` (`series` takes a `ScenarioResponse` or an `ActionImpact`; also `scenarioSeries`/`impactSeries`), `CoachCardPresenter`.

- `AnswerPresentation.ts` (web addition): `AnswerPresenter.view(message, actions)` (the one result, the details, merged assumptions, source chips), `CardText.humanize`, `SourceTags`, `messageActions`. `WatchModels.ts`: the watch (Projects) models with tolerant decoders and `projectStatusOf`.
- `AppLogic/Conversation.ts`: the hands-free conversation (port of `Conversation.swift` of the three-tab iOS app): `SpeechEndpointer` (hears when a question starts and ends from the microphone level alone; the noise floor follows the room. Hardened on the web after QA, 2026-11-14: readings at or below -150 dBFS, the analyser's "no audio yet", teach it nothing, and the room is measured from the first real reading; the threshold stays 8 dB above the median of the quiet room; a pause is a level under the threshold minus 4 dB or 18 dB under the speaker's own smoothed level, and only 0.1 s of renewed loudness ends a pause, so background voices neither start nor prolong a question; `minimumSpeech` is 0.33 s so a one-word "No." counts while a cough does not. iOS keeps the original rules), `ConversationFlow` (the state machine: off, listening, hearing, thinking, speaking; every method takes the time and returns `ConversationCommand`s: `openMicrophone`, `discardRecording`, `sendRecording`, `stopSisu`), `ConversationPresenter` (what the Sisu tab writes: title, detail, Liito's mood, button labels, the status line `Conversation on · 0:42` / `Ended · 2 questions · 1:40`, the level ring), `ConversationText`. **Exception to the immutable rule:** `SpeechEndpointer` and `ConversationFlow` are small mutable classes owned by one controller (like `VoiceClipQueue`); `copy()` gives the value that is published to the screen.
- `LiitoTask.ts` (web addition, no Swift counterpart yet): `LiitoTask` = `listening | writing | searching | calculating | comparing | presenting`, the six task moods appended to `LiitoMood`. `LiitoTask.next(task, event)` follows the agent's real events (account and web tools and extraction → searching; affordability, scenarios, calculation stages → calculating; offers found, offer or peer charts → comparing; other charts, impact, `show_chart`, `explain_concept` → presenting; `prepare_action`, `remember`, text deltas → writing; the early `speculation` search → searching; start and action → none). `sentence` events mean nothing on their own (the server sends them in silent typed turns too): presenting comes from real speech and from charts. It only sequences moods that real events produced. The badges expose `data-mood` (shown) and `data-mood-target` (what the events say now). Under reduced motion Liito lands each pose, then holds still (`stillAfterMs` on `RiveComponent`). `CoachSession` keeps `task` in its state (reset when a turn starts, ends or is cancelled) and has `liitoMood`; `PlanView` has `task`.
- `AppLogic/`: `AppPresenters` (`NumberText`, `TodayPresenter`, `TodayFocus`), `WeeklyBudget` (+ `WeeklyBudgetText`), `SisuSuggestions`, `VoiceBar`, `CoachAppLogic` (`CoachBillMatch.billType(billOrText)`, `CoachMood`, `CoachProviderName`), `DayStamp`, `LessonPath` (`LessonProgress`, `LessonNode`, `LessonPathPresenter`, `LessonWording`), `LessonFollowUps` (`LessonAttempt`), `Onboarding` (`OnboardingStep`, `OnboardingLaunch.fromArgument`, `OnboardingFlow`), `OnboardingCopy` (`OnboardingCopy`, `OnboardingProfiles`, `OnboardingReading`, `FirstInsightPresenter`), `SampleEvidence`, `VoiceLogic` (`VoicePressRule`, `VoiceButtonPresenter`, `VoiceText`, `VoiceClipQueue`, `VoiceAudioFormat` with `Uint8Array` audio).

## web/core (part A): models, API, transport, streams, coach and plan sessions

Port of `ios/SisuCore/Sources/SisuCore/{Models,API,Transport,ServerSentEvents,Activity,CoachModels,CoachEndpoints,CoachClient,CoachSession,PlanModels,PlanSession}.swift`, one `.ts` per Swift file, same names. Pure TypeScript, no React. Import with `@/web/core/<File>`.

**Conventions used in these files (follow them when you read or extend them).**
- A Swift struct is an `interface` whose fields are the JSON keys. Swift optionals are `field?: T | null` (the server sends `null`). Its methods, computed properties and initialisers live in a **companion `const` with the same name**: `CoachMessage.make(id, role, fields)`, `CoachMessage.apply(message, event)`, `SisuActivity.make(state, label?, detail?)`, `SisuActivity.idle`, `SisuActivity.mood(a)`, `PlanView.applying(view, change)`, `PlanView.waitingActions(view)`, `PlanTotals.spendingChangeYearlyEur(t)`, `PlanItem.needsSpendingChange(i)`, `CustomerMemory.confirmedBillName(m, billId)`, `TurnTimings.usedBackup(t)`, `WarmResponse.server(w)`, `ImpactBranch.branchId(b)`. Request bodies whose Swift `init` has defaults have a `make`: `AgentRequest.make` (mode `text`), `InvestigateRequest.make` (mode `text`), `SpeechRequest.make` (format `mp3`; without it the server defaults to `pcm`), `ApproveAllRequest.make` (confirm `true`).
- A Swift `enum: String` is a string union whose values are the raw values, plus a companion with the case names and `allCases`: `SisuActivityState.readingAccount === "reading_account"`, `SisuActivityState.pose(s)`, `SisuActivityState.defaultLabel(s)`, `LiitoPose.mood(p)`, `BasketId.fromRaw(text)`.
- Swift "open enums" (`BillType`, `ActionKind`, `ActionStatus`, `MemoryKind`, `StageKind`, `ConceptId`, `ResearchStatus`, `AffordabilityVerdict`) are `"known" | (string & {})` types with named constants: `BillType.electricity`, `ActionStatus.awaitingApproval`, `StageKind.investigationOrder`, `ConceptId.all`.
- A Swift enum with associated values is a discriminated union on **`kind` = the Swift case name**, its associated labels as fields (unlabelled ones take the wire key): `SisuEvent` (`start`, `transcript`, `speculation`, `toolStart`, `toolEnd {trace}`, `filler {text}`, `research {result}`, `delta {text}`, `sentence {index,text}`, `stage {stage}`, `action {action}`, `teaching {card}`, `memory {item}`, `grounding {grounding,facts}`, `visual {visual}`, `replace {text}`, `done {text,citations,timings}`, `error {message}`, `unknown {type}`); `PlanEvent` (`planStart`, `scan {scan}`, `finding {finding}`, `searchStart`, `searchPages`, `pageRead {page}`, `offers {offers}`, `checkStart`, `checkDone {check}`, `calc {steps,totals}`, `action`, `projection`, `summary {text,by}`, `planDone {plan}`, `error`, `unknown {type}`); `StageData` (the wire shape, plus `{kind:"unknown", rawKind}`); `Visual.content: VisualContent` (`budgetDonut`, `peerBars`, `monthBars`, `waterfall`, `futuresLines`, `offerBars`, `unknown`; `visual.kind` stays the wire string such as `budget_donut`). `SisuEvent.type(e)` / `PlanEvent.type(e)` give the wire `type`. `JSONValue` (tool args) is plain JSON.
- Swift namespaces (`enum Endpoints`, `CoachEndpoints`, `ScenarioEngine`, `Activity`, `CoachErrors`, `CoachSamples`) are classes with static members: `Activity.nextActivity(current, event)`, `Activity.nextPlanActivity(current, event)`, `Activity.stateForTool(name)`, `CoachErrors.sentence(error)`, `ScenarioEngine.run(overview, input)`.
- `SisuError` is a throwable class; switch on `error.info.kind` (`server {status,message}`, `offline`, `invalidResponse`, `unknownCustomer {customerId}`, `invalidState`, `notImplemented`). `SisuError.status(e)` gives a server status or null. Swift's `CancellationError` is `CancellationError`; `isCancellation(e)` also accepts an `AbortError`. Show errors with `CoachErrors.sentence(error)` (cancellation gives `""`).
- Byte payloads are `Uint8Array`; recordings and photos sent up are `Blob | Uint8Array` (`FormBytes`). `SpeechAudio` is `{ data: Uint8Array, contentType }` (play by `contentType`: the mock server answers `audio/wav`).
- `URLRequest` is `SisuRequest { url, method, headers, body (JSON text or FormData), timeoutInterval (seconds) }`, built by `Endpoints.*` and `CoachEndpoints.*` (pure; JSON bodies have sorted keys and drop null members, like Swift's `JSONEncoder`).

**The data sources.**
```ts
import { CoachClient } from "@/web/core/CoachClient";
import { FixtureAPI, LiveAPI } from "@/web/core/API";
const live = CoachClient.live();                       // same origin: relative /api/... URLs (base "")

await live.overview("CUST04974");                      // CoachClient also implements SisuAPI (demoCustomers, overview, transactions, scenario)
new LiveAPI(""); new FixtureAPI();                     // the plain SisuAPI implementations of API.swift
```
`client.kind` is `"live"` or `"bundledSample"`; `client.fallbackNotice` / `fallenBackAt` say when a fallback happened (`CoachClient.fallbackSentence`). Every `CoachClient` call of Swift is here with the same name and argument order (optional Swift labels became optional trailing parameters), e.g. `research(customerId, billType, wait = true, yearlyKwh = null)`, `teachingCards(customerId, concept = null)`, `mortgage(customerId)` (null on 404), `approveAll(customerId, ids)` (a 409 still returns its `failed`), `speech(SpeechRequest.make({ text }))`, `transcribe(blob, fileName, mimeType, language)`, `readBillPhoto(customerId, blob)`.

Sample files are fetched at runtime from `/sisu-fixtures/<path>` (copied from `ios/SisuCore/Sources/SisuCore/Fixtures`, never bundled into JS) through a `FixtureSource = (path) => Promise<Uint8Array | null>`; `FixtureAPI.setDefaultSource(src)` swaps it (the checks read the files from disk). `ReplayTransport.fixture("coach/warm.json")` reads one file. `ReplayTransport` keeps the session's approvals and memory in memory, like iOS.

**Streams.** The agent and plan endpoints are POSTs, so they are read with `fetch` + `ReadableStream` (not `EventSource`) and parsed by `SSEParser` / `SisuEventStreamParser` (any chunking, CR/LF/CRLF, comments, multi-line data). Streams are **async generators**:
```ts
const controller = new AbortController();
for await (const event of client.ask(AgentRequest.make({ customerId, text, history }), controller.signal)) {
  if (event.kind === "delta") …
}
// break out of the loop or controller.abort() to close the connection; an HTTP error or a drop throws SisuError
```
`client.investigate(InvestigateRequest.make({ customerId }), signal)`, `client.voiceTurn(customerId, blob, fileName, mimeType, history, focusBill, language, signal)` and `client.plan(customerId, fresh, language, signal)` work the same. Timeouts are Swift's (5 s quick, 15 s research, 30 s voice, 90 s streams) and, as with `URLRequest`, count the longest wait between two pieces of data.

**Sessions (the screens' state).** `CoachSession` (one per customer: conversation, actions, memory, activity) and `PlanSession` (the money plan "director") are stores for React's `useSyncExternalStore(session.subscribe, session.getSnapshot)`. The snapshot is immutable and replaced on every change: `CoachSessionState { messages, server, actions, memory, isThinking, isRecording, isSpeaking, heardLanguage, notice, fallbackNotice, working, moment }` and `PlanSessionState { view, fallbackNotice }`. Derived values are getters on the session: `status`, `activity` (drive the mascot with `SisuActivity.mood(session.activity)`), `approvedMonthlySaving`, `dataLabel` (`live` / `mock` / `sample`), `isSample`; `plan.view`, `plan.dataLabel`.

const session = new CoachSession(customerId, client, { speech, language: "auto", focusBill: null }); // or CoachSession.sample(id)

`SpeechOutput` is `{ speak(text, order), stop() }` (implemented in web/voice); with one set, `speakReplies` defaults to true and every `filler` and `sentence` is passed to it in order. `CoachSamples.events(turn, id)`, `reply`, `research`, `action`, `teachingCards` give recorded payloads (async on the web).

**Checks.** `npx tsx web/core/__checks__/core-a.check.ts` (from the repo root): SSE parsing in every chunk size, all recorded streams decode with no unknown events, investigations fill the seven stages, the offline `ScenarioEngine` reproduces the recorded server scenarios to 0.01, request building, the mock-backend question routing, the sample client, a whole coach session and a whole plan with approve-all, and the live client over a mocked `fetch` (chunked stream, HTTP error, offline, fallback).

**Watches (Projects), 2026-11-15.** `web/core/WatchModels.ts` mirrors the watch types of `lib/types.ts` on main (`Listing`, `WatchPlan`, `WatchedPage`, `PropertyWatch`, `WatchNotification`, `WatchList`, `WatchCheck`) with every field optional, plus fields the backend does not send yet (`goal`, `requirements`, status `paused`/`failing`, `lastSuccessAt`, `nextCheckAt`, `read`, `housingEffect`); each has a tolerant `decode` (wrong types dropped, arrays always arrays, entries without an `id` skipped). `AgentAction` has `watch` (raw: read it with `WatchPlan.decode`) and `execution` (unknown). ## Rive (web/rive)

Port of `ios/Sisu/Rive`. The web loads the very same `sisu_ui.riv` (copied to `public/rive/sisu_ui.riv`; re-copy it whenever `ios/Sisu/Resources/Rive/sisu_ui.riv` changes) with `@rive-app/react-canvas` and binds each artboard's view model by name, as `rive/contract.json` says.

- `RiveLibrary.tsx`: `<RiveProvider>` loads the file once (`useRiveFile`) and shares it. Put it once near the app root (the lab has its own). A failed load is remembered and every component of the file draws its placeholder. Without a provider components draw placeholders (and warn once in development).
- `RiveComponent.tsx`: the only component screens need. Same rules as `RiveComponentView`:

```tsx
import { RiveComponent } from "@/web/rive/RiveComponent";
import { RiveBindings } from "@/web/core/RiveContract";

<RiveComponent component="HeroNumber" values={RiveBindings.heroNumber(hero)} />

<RiveComponent component="Button" values={RiveBindings.button("Continue")}
  onTrigger={(name) => { /* name === "pressed" */ }} />

<RiveComponent component="Jar" values={RiveBindings.jar(0.4, "Buffer")}
  triggers={[`celebrate#${celebrations}`]} style={{ height: 200 }} />

<RiveComponent component="Liito" values={RiveBindings.liito("idle")} sizing="contain" style={{ width: 96, height: 96 }} />
```

  - `values`: the artboard's `in` properties by name (`RiveValue` from web/core: `{ kind: "string" | "number" | "bool" | "enumCase", value }`; plain strings/numbers/booleans work too, a plain string goes to a string or enum property of that name). Written when the artboard loads (before its first frame) and whenever one changes; only changed properties are rewritten, compared by content, so building the object on every render is fine. Set them all (`RiveBindings` / `web/shared/RiveValues` build complete sets). A name or type the artboard does not have is skipped with a `[Rive]` debug line.
  - `triggers`: requests to fire `in` triggers, `"name"` or `"name#stamp"` (`RiveTriggerRequest.ts`). The first array fires nothing; afterwards every new entry fires once; requests made while loading wait for the artboard.
  - `onTrigger(name)`: each `out` trigger the artboard fires (`pressed`, `tap0`…, `toggled`, `done`, `pressDown`/`pressUp`). `listening={["name"]}` adds triggers the contract does not list. Firing a trigger you also listen to calls `onTrigger` too.
  - Size from the contract (`sizing.ts`): `fixed` = design size in px; `flexibleWidth` = 100% wide at the design height (the artboard re-lays out to the width, 1 unit = 1 px); `contain` = design size, never wider than its parent, aspect ratio kept (give it a width and height to scale it); `fill` = 100% x 100% of the parent. `sizing="contain"` replaces the component's own (like `.sizing(.contain)`); `className`/`style` come last, so they are the `.frame(...)`. Canvases render at the device pixel ratio (capped at 3).
  - Placeholder: a pale-mint rounded block with the component name when the file or artboard cannot load; with exactly one contract `out` trigger and an `onTrigger`, tapping it fires that trigger.
  - Accessibility: `ariaLabel`, `ariaValue`, `isButton`, `onActivate` (see `riveAccessible` in shared). `testId` sets `data-testid`; every component also carries `data-rive="<Artboard>"` and `data-rive-phase="loading|ready|unavailable"`.
  - Performance: one shared file, the offscreen renderer, rendering paused while a canvas is off screen (IntersectionObserver), instances and trigger listeners cleaned up on unmount. Touch drags that start on a canvas still scroll the page (`touchScroll`, default true; false for `VoiceButton`, whose press-and-hold must own the touch).
- `contract.ts`: `riveContract("Button")` (design size, sizing, in/out triggers, properties, purpose), `allRiveContracts`, `riveEnums`, `RiveComponents.button` etc. Read from `rive/contract.json`; the types are web/core's.
- `RiveComponentModel.ts`: the non-React model (values, written cache, trigger queue, listeners), like the Swift one.
- `/app/lab` (`app/app/lab`): every artboard with its contract defaults, buttons for `in` triggers, enum pickers and a log of `out` triggers; then the shared pieces (button, icon button, pressable card, notice, sheet, glass tab bar). No gate.

**`CoachStore`** (`web/app/CoachStore.ts`). Snapshot `{ session, voice, conversation, fellBackToSample, quizChoices, deciding, decisionNotices, cheering }`. `conversation` is the Sisu tab's `ConversationController` for the session, made with the session and shut down on `clear()` and when the session is replaced; it shares the voice controller's `SpeechPlayer`, and its `repliesMuted` follows the voice controller's mute. Methods: `quiz(card)` → `TeachingQuiz` with the pick applied; `choose(index, card)` → false if already answered; `isDeciding(id)`; `decide(actionId, approve)` → `Promise<AgentAction | null>` (failure sentence in `decisionNotices[id]`; an executed approval sets `cheering`); `setCheering(false)` when a new turn starts thinking; `ask(text)`, `investigate()` (typed turns, silent unless the voice controller speaks typed replies); `voiceDown()`, `voiceUp()`, `voiceState`; `configure(customerId, baseURL | null, unreachable)`, `clear()`, `resetDemo()`; history: `newConversation()`, `openConversation(id)`, `deleteConversation(id)`, `clearHistory()` (snapshot `conversationId`, `savedConversations`); `setLanguage(code)`; `dataTag`, `fallenBackAt` getters. `approvedMonthlySaving` on the app follows the session's actions.

**Voice** (`web/voice`). `VoiceController` (state `{ state, notice, clipsPlayed, isMuted, speakTypedReplies, isRecording }`): `pressDown()`, `pressUp()`, `handle(trigger)` (the VoiceButton's `pressDown`/`pressUp`), `toggleFromAccessibility()`, `accessibilityLabel`, `setMuted(b)`, `setSpeakTypedReplies(b)`, `setNotice(null)`, `beginTypedTurn()`. Give the Rive `VoiceButton` `touchScroll={false}`. `VoiceRecorder` records with MediaRecorder (`audio/webm;codecs=opus`, else `audio/mp4` on iOS Safari, else ogg), 60 s limit, interrupted when the page is hidden or the track ends; the microphone opens per press. `SpeechPlayer` fetches each sentence from `POST /api/voice/tts` (mp3) and plays them in order through one Web Audio context; `AudioUnlock.ts` unlocks it on any tap (mobile autoplay rule). `VoiceLabScreen` (default export) is the lab; PathScreen shows it when `VoiceLaunch.current().voiceLab`.

**Hands-free conversation** (`web/voice/ConversationController.ts`, port of `ConversationController.swift`). A store with `{ flow, level, starts, ends, answerReady, notice }` (`notice`: speech failed or a microphone problem, shown above the Sisu tab's ask box; cleared by the next Start or question); `toggle()` (Start / End), `start()`, `end()`, `toggleMute()`, `interrupt()` (tapping Liito while Sisu answers), `newConversation()` (also resets the question count), `listenForAgain()` ("Try again" on an unheard voice question), `setHeld(bool)` (the Sisu tab sets it while a project covers the conversation, while the tab is not mounted and while the page is hidden), `screen(activity, opening)` (the `ConversationScreenState` with the session's task), `shutDown()`. It only carries out `ConversationFlow`'s commands: the microphone is opened **per question** (`VoiceRecorder.prepare()` + `start(true)`) and closed while Sisu answers (iOS Safari ducks or reroutes playback while a microphone track is live, and Sisu must not hear itself); the level is read every 50 ms; a finished question goes through `session.sendVoice(…, VoicePrefs.recogniserLanguage())` (the backend's `/api/voice/turn`, with the "I speak" language as push-to-talk sends it); when the stream has ended and the `SpeechPlayer` is drained (`onDrained`, `isIdle`), it listens again. A typed question is never cut off: coming back while it runs, the microphone waits until it is done. Fixture mode (`voicefixture=1`) "hears" the bundled clip once: quiet, about a second of simulated speech after `talkFixtureDelay`, then quiet, so the real endpointer ends the question. Haptics with `navigator.vibrate` where it exists. **Metering:** `VoiceRecorder.start(true)` puts an `AnalyserNode` on the shared `AudioContext` (never connected to the speakers) and `level()` returns 20·log10(RMS) in dBFS (-160 silence), like `AVAudioRecorder.averagePower`; the Start tap unlocks the context. `stop()` lets go of its own stream at once, so the next `prepare()` gets a fresh one; concurrent `prepare()` calls share one request.
