# 02 · Identity, personality and mascot

Status: v0.2, 2026-11-13 (colours now follow the S-mobiili app). The name and the mascot are proposals (see [`00-brief.md`](00-brief.md), "Still open").

## Positioning

**For** 18 to 35 year olds who never learned to budget,
**Sisu is** the money coach inside S-Pankki
**that** turns your own transactions into one small win a day,
**unlike** budgeting apps that need setup and bank apps that only show lists,
**because** it already has your data, it is free, and it never earns from your debt.

One line: **Small wins, every day, with your own money.**

White space we take (from [`research/competitor-analysis.md`](../research/competitor-analysis.md)): no product is at once playful, daily, mascot-led, built on real transactions, in Finnish, free, and clean of credit sales.

## Name

**Sisu.** Chosen by the team on 2026-11-13. It replaces the earlier working name.

- Written lowercase in the logo: **sisu**.
- Always shown with the parent on first contact: "sisu by S-Pankki".
- Not checked for trademarks.

### The three baskets

The brief's teaching angle. Each basket has a fixed name, colour and icon everywhere in the product.

| Brief wording | Name | Finnish (proposed) | Colour | Icon | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mandatory and regular | **Fixed** | Kiinteät | Dark green `#004628` | Anchor | Rent, phone, insurance |
| Mandatory and irregular | **Curveballs** | Yllärit | Deep green `#007841` | Lightning | Dentist, repairs, annual fees |
| Everyday that slips away | **Leaks** | Vuodot | S green `#00AA46` | Drop | Coffee, delivery, subscriptions |

The darker the green, the more fixed the money. Jars (savings) are mint, the lightest step, and are the fourth thing the user sees next to the baskets.

## Personality

Sisu is **the friend who is good with money and never makes you feel small**.

| Trait | Means | Does not mean |
|---|---|---|
| **Playful** | Jokes, stickers, small celebrations | Clowning, memes about being broke |
| **Straight** | Real numbers first, plain words | Blunt, cold, a wall of figures |
| **On your side** | Cheers effort, protects tight weeks | Flattering, hiding bad news |
| **Calm about money** | Lowers stress, shows the next step | Vague, "everything is fine" |
| **Finnish at heart** | Forest, berries, sauna-level honesty | Flags, clichés, lakeside stock photos |

## What makes it unmistakable

Seven things. A screen that uses at least four of them looks like Sisu.

1. **S-Pankki's green world, turned up.** The app's own greens on its own pale green surface, with dark green hero blocks and mint numbers. Nobody mistakes it for another bank.
2. **Buttons that press down.** Deep green face, white label, a dark green edge underneath.
3. **Liito.** A grey flying squirrel with huge eyes and a green-lined glide.
4. **Jars on a shelf.** Savings goals are jam jars that fill.
5. **The receipt.** Weekly recaps look like a shop receipt with a zigzag edge.
6. **Stickers.** Badges and milestones are die-cut stickers with a white border, tilted a few degrees.
7. **One big number.** Every main screen leads with a single heavy figure.

## Mascot: Liito

**Species:** Siberian flying squirrel (liito-orava), a protected species that lives in Finnish forests. "Liito" means glide.

**Why this animal**

- Squirrels put food away for winter, which is the saving story without a piggy bank. *Check the detail (flying squirrels storing catkins) before using it as a fact in copy.*
- "Oravanpyörä" (squirrel wheel) is the Finnish word for the rat race. Liito is the squirrel that got out.
- The real animal has very large dark eyes, so emotion reads at small sizes.
- It glides. That gives a signature pose and a motion language: soft landings, gliding to payday.
- No Finnish bank uses one. (France's Caisse d'Épargne uses a red squirrel logo; ours is grey, a different species and a character, not a logo.)

**Design spec**

| Part | Rule |
|---|---|
| Silhouette | A rounded dumpling. Head and body are one shape, about 1 : 1.1 wide to tall. |
| Eyes | Very large, round, near-black (`#0B1F16`), one white highlight each. About one third of the face width each. |
| Fur | Soft warm grey `#B9C2BD`, lighter belly and face `#F4F1E8`. |
| Glide membrane | Stretches between front and back paws. Outside grey, **inside S green `#00AA46`**. Only visible when arms are out. |
| Ears | Small and round. They carry mood: up, flat, one down. |
| Tail | Flat and wide, like a feather. Curls round the body at rest. |
| Cheeks | Round. A small mint `#91FA9B` glow when proud or shy. |
| Mouth | Tiny. Most emotion comes from eyes, ears and arms. |
| Style | Flat vector, no outline, two tones per colour at most, no gradients, no 3D render. |
| Clothes | None. Props only: a jar, a berry, a receipt, a mug, a blanket. |

**Moods.** Liito's mood follows the user's **effort**, never their balance.

| Mood | When | Pose |
|---|---|---|
| Cheering | An action is done | Arms up, membrane open, green showing |
| Proud | A streak milestone, a full jar | Holding a jar, blush, eyes closed in a smile |
| Gliding | Loading, moving between steps, payday | Full spread, seen from the front |
| Curious | Reading the user's question in chat | Head tilted, one ear down |
| Found something | A leak or an unused subscription is spotted | Wide eyes, holding a magnifier or a drop |
| Focused | A lesson | Small, leaning in, a berry in hand |
| Cosy | Tight-week mode | Wrapped in a blanket with a mug |
| Sleepy | The streak is waiting today | Yawning on a branch. Never sad. |

**Never:** angry, disappointed, crying, begging, shocked at a balance, holding cash, wearing a suit, or pointing at the user.

**Where Liito appears**

- Chat avatar and the voice of every chat reply.
- Onboarding questions and permission requests.
- Celebrations, empty states and the streak.
- One small appearance on home. Never more than one Liito on a screen.

**Where Liito does not appear:** transaction lists, payment confirmations, error messages about money, and legal text.
